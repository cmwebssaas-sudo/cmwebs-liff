## Waiting for rolling slots before metadata debit

The scoped release control also provides `waitForExternalReadSlot(units)`.
Await readiness, then await `reserveExternalReads(units)`, then perform exactly
the declared IO. Readiness alone spends nothing and never grants a reservation.
The public driver method can wait out conservative preparation minute slots
before a new run, but must not be called recursively from a locked observer.
Direct reserve/run methods retain their existing over-limit STOP behavior.

Waits retain the epoch lock, compute the first sufficiently expiring batch,
and last at most60000ms per call. They do not retry an IO or rejected debit.
After the wait, revalidate the live private journal/registry, total40, date,
expiry and full required minute headroom. An impossible total, predicted expiry
or Taipei-day crossing stops before sleep; early/frozen-clock return, changed
journal or pause failure stops after it. An observer cannot swallow failure or
return while its readiness promise remains pending; a late rejection is consumed.

Local pause injection is for the trusted clock adapter; it is not a sandbox for
arbitrary callback IO. The actual observer must order and debit its declared
Google/native reads, leave the next operation's full minute headroom, and supply
